## What's new
Team and Enterprise plans can tag Claude directly in Slack conversations and delegate tasks to it while they focus on other work.

## When is it safe to use
Announced without a beta label for Team and Enterprise plans. Claude works in the Slack channels where it is tagged, so confirm your admins allow it for the channels you use.

## Use case and value
Example: a teammate tags Claude in a thread to draft a summary of the discussion while the team moves on.

## How to implement
Tag Claude in a Slack conversation. See the Introducing Claude Tag post linked from the [apps release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes).
