## What's new
Claude Cowork is now available on web and mobile as well as desktop. It runs sessions remotely, so your sessions and files are saved to your Claude account and go where you go. Work continues when you close your laptop, and scheduled tasks run with no device online. Chat and Cowork share one home with one place for projects and artifacts. The same day, the Microsoft 365 connector gained write tools to draft, send and organize email, manage calendar events and update files in OneDrive and SharePoint.

## When is it safe to use
Remote sessions are in beta, and the rollout starts with the Max plan, so it may not be on your plan yet. Files and sessions are saved to your Claude account.

## Use case and value
Example: start a report on your laptop, close it, and check progress from your phone while Claude keeps working.

## How to implement
No setup is described. Look for Cowork on web or mobile once your plan is reached; see Use Claude Cowork in the [apps release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes).
