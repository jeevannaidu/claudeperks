## What's new
Text generated by Claude Opus 4.6, Claude Opus 4.5, Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Claude Haiku 4.5 carries Anthropic's text watermark on supported platforms. The docs say it adds no tokens or hidden characters and does not change meaning, quality or readability.

## When is it safe to use
Generally available, going by the docs' wording, which gives no beta label. You do not need to change requests or response handling. If a request sets `temperature`, `top_p` or `top_k` to a non-default value, the text might not carry the watermark.

## Use case and value
Example: a team that publishes AI-written text and wants it marked as AI-generated can rely on the watermark. It needs no extra step in their pipeline.

## How to implement
Nothing to turn on. If you rely on the watermark, omit `temperature`, `top_p` and `top_k` from your requests. Also check that your framework or client library does not set them for you. Docs: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/text-watermark
