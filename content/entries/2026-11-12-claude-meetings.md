## What's new
Claude Meetings lets you add Claude to a Google Meet call as a visible participant. It takes notes, answers questions and builds artifacts such as docs, whiteboards and slides during the call. It is rolling out in Claude and Claude Desktop on Team and Enterprise plans.

## When is it safe to use
Beta. It is limited to Team and Enterprise plans and is still rolling out, so expect gaps. Claude joins as a visible participant, so everyone on the call can see it is there.

## Use case and value
Example: a project lead adds Claude to a weekly review call and leaves with notes and a slide summary already drafted, instead of writing them up afterwards.

## How to implement
On a Team or Enterprise plan, open Claude or Claude Desktop and add Claude to a Google Meet call. Full steps are in the Use Claude Meetings page linked from the [apps release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes).
