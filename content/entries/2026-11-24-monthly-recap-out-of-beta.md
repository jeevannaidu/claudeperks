## What's new
The monthly recap (Reflect, in Settings) is out of beta. It is on the web, Claude Desktop, and Claude for iOS and Android. Recaps can now draw on your Claude Code sessions and Claude Cowork tasks in the cloud. You can also delete your stored recaps from the page.

## When is it safe to use
Generally available on Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra plans. It is not available on Team or Enterprise plans. The recap only appears when memory is turned on. The help article says the rollout is gradual, so you may not see it yet.

## Use case and value
Example: a developer opens the recap at month end to see which topics took the most time and which habits are working. It gives a view of your own usage without you tracking it by hand.

## How to implement
Turn memory on. Open Claude on the web or Claude Desktop, go to Settings > Wellbeing, and under Reflect select "View". On iOS or Android, open Settings and tap "Reflect". See [See your monthly recap](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15672559-see-your-monthly-recap).
