## What's new
The monthly recap (Reflect, in Settings) is out of beta for Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans. It works on the web, Claude Desktop, and Claude for iOS and Android. Recaps can now draw on your Claude Code sessions and Claude Cowork tasks in the cloud, and you can delete your stored recaps from the page.

## When is it safe to use
Out of beta (generally available) on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans. The notes say you can delete your stored recaps. They do not mention Team or Enterprise plans.

## Use case and value
Example: at month end you open your recap to see what you worked on across chats, Claude Code and Cowork, instead of searching old conversations.

## How to implement
Open Settings and find Reflect to see your monthly recap. Steps are in the See your monthly recap article linked from the [apps release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes).
