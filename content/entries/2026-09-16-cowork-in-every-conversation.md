## What's new
A new Claude experience makes everything Claude Cowork does available from any conversation. You can ask a quick question or hand over a bigger task, such as a report, spreadsheet or presentation, without choosing a mode first. Your chats, Cowork tasks, projects, connectors and skills carry over. On the same day, designs, decks and documents became available in any conversation, including Claude Code and the Artifacts tab, through Claude Design, Claude Slides and Claude Docs.

## When is it safe to use
Rolling out gradually to Pro and Max plans on web, desktop and mobile, so it may not be on your account yet. Existing chats and Cowork tasks carry over.

## Use case and value
Example: you ask a question in chat, then ask Claude to turn the answer into a slide deck in the same conversation.

## How to implement
No setup is described. When the rollout reaches your account, start a conversation and ask for the task; see Claude Cowork and chat are one Claude, linked from the [apps release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes).
