## What's new
The monthly recap (called Reflect, in Settings) is out of beta on the web, Claude Desktop, and Claude for iOS and Android. Recaps can now draw on your Claude Code sessions and Claude Cowork tasks in the cloud. You can also delete your stored recaps from the page.

## When is it safe to use
Generally available on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans. Stored recaps can be deleted from the page.

## Use case and value
Example: at month end you open the recap to see what you worked on across chats, Claude Code and Cowork.

## How to implement
Open Reflect in Settings. See the See your monthly recap article linked from the [apps release notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes).
